Services

Three ways to get found, and get chosen

Take one, or all three — most local businesses need all three working together to actually show up in search.

Search Engine Optimization

Keyword research, on-page fixes, content structure, and technical clean-up so search engines can find your site — and rank it.

Google Business Profile

Setup or clean-up, categories, service areas, photos, posts, and a review strategy so you show up — and look credible — in the map pack.

Web Design

Fast, mobile-first sites built around one job: turning a visit into a call, a booking, or an order — not just looking good.

Process

How a project runs

Same four stages whether it's a full site build or a Google Business Profile clean-up.

1

Audit

Review your current site, listing, and search visibility to find what's actually costing you customers.

2

Plan

Prioritize fixes by impact — quick wins first, structural work next — and agree the scope together.

3

Build

Implement the SEO fixes, set up the profile, or build the site, with check-ins along the way.

4

Track

Monthly reporting on rankings, calls, and traffic, with adjustments as the data comes in.
